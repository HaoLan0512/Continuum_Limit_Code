Interpretation: seed 3 is an intentionally selected illustrative phase-stress realization, not representative random-start statistics.
Its finite-grid sawtooth is assessed by an O(h) envelope; smooth pointwise halving is not claimed.
Accepted endpoints are stationary candidates with no resolved negative curvature at the raw endpoint. Global minimality and the paper's stability-gap hypothesis are not verified.
